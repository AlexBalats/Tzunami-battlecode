# Ryth

![Ryth](<Ryth.png>)

## Facts (from `GameRules.md`)

A melee champion with an area stun and an attack reset. Health also adds Strength.
- **Passive:** Ryth gains Strength equal to 1% of bonus health.
- **Slot 0, Area stun:** 1 × Focus damage and a one-round stun in radius one. *(Target / range: Ground · 4. Walking: Excludes walking. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Slot 1, Attack reset:** Resets your basic attack cooldown. *(Target / range: Self. Walking: Compatible. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Stats:** 1200 HP / 50 Str / 100 Focus. Per shard: +100 / +5 / +10
- **Type:** Melee, range 1

## Our take

**Role (one word):** Tank

### Key numbers (base stats, before items)

- Damage per 8-turn cycle **[rules-derived]**: 50 × 8 + 50 (reset) + 100 (stun) = 550, **~69/turn** (mid-pack).
- Joint-highest HP (1200, with Marcella).
- Solo wolf **[estimate]**: ~20 turns, takes ~20 × 20 = 400 damage. Comfortable.
- Solo Elk **[estimate]**: ~35 turns at 60 Str = ~2100 damage. Not survivable alone at base.
- Duel vs Vendetta **[estimate]**: Vendetta needs ~15 turns to kill Ryth, Ryth needs ~12. Ryth wins.

**Strengths:**
- **Safest gem carrier on the roster:** hardest body to kill, so he risks bags least (P3).
- **Health shards count twice:** +100 HP and +1 Str each (passive). Health + Durability emblem = very tough tank.
- **Area stun can cancel two channels at once,** e.g. two enemies tributing at the same altar.
- **Area damage hits connected camp monsters,** slightly better on camp groups.
- **Strong at chokepoints** (P17).

**Weaknesses:**
- **No mobility:** no dash, can't chase or escape, loses races to altars and camps.
- **Low burst:** poor at finishing kills.
- **Stun excludes walking:** using it while chasing costs a step.
- **Marcella is immune to his stun.**

**Best moment:** Holding a chokepoint or altar, stunning two enemies at once while allies follow up.

**Needs:** Health shards (double value) and a Durability emblem. Ranged allies to deal the damage.

**Good with:** Ranged damage (Stellar, Tavi) to follow up the stun; Cassi for a 2-round stun chain.

**Bad against:** Marcella (stun immune); slippery dash champions (Vesper Vale, Vendetta, Ren) that can disengage.

**Draft value:** Safe, flexible first pick that fits any composition.

**Art of War angle:**
- P3: make yourself unbeatable first; the safest carrier.
- P17: use the terrain; chokepoints make the area stun count.
- P19: the anchor that ranged allies fight around.
