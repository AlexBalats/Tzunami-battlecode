# Marcella

![Marcella](<Marcella.png>)

## Facts (from `GameRules.md`)

A melee champion immune to stuns, with a stunning enhanced attack and a ranged damage spell.
- **Passive:** Immune to stuns
- **Slot 0, Enhanced attack reset:** Resets your basic attack cooldown; the next enhanced attack stuns for one round. *(Target / range: Self. Walking: Compatible. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Slot 1, Single-target damage:** 2 × Focus damage. *(Target / range: Enemy or camp · 4. Walking: Excludes walking. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Stats:** 1200 HP / 45 Str / 50 Focus. Per shard: +100 / +5 / +10
- **Type:** Melee, range 1

## Our take

**Role (one word):** Anchor

### Key numbers (base stats, before items)

- Damage per 8-turn cycle **[rules-derived]**: 45 × 9 + 100 = 505, **~63/turn**. Modest.
- Burst: attack + enhanced attack = 90 **plus a stun**; nuke adds 100 poke at range 4.
- Scaling: Strength shard ~5.6/turn; Focus shard only ~2.5/turn (+20 nuke per cycle). **Buy Strength and Health.**
- Duel vs Vendetta **[estimate]**: Vendetta needs ~15 turns, Marcella ~13. Marcella wins.
- Duel vs Ryth **[estimate]**: about even (~17 vs ~19 turns); his stun still damages but doesn't lock her.

**Strengths:**
- **Safest tributer in the game.** Damage doesn't interrupt tribute and stuns can't touch her, so **only an altar lock or killing her (1200 HP) stops the channel.** Same for teleport crystals. Natural gem carrier, better than Ryth (P3).
- **Hard counter to stun teams:** Cassi, Ryth, Prism and Tavi's stuns do nothing to her.
- **Melee stun:** cancels an enemy channel up close, or holds a target for a Stellar nuke / Vendetta dive.
- **Tanky; wins most melee duels.**

**Weaknesses:**
- **Low damage** for a frontliner; low base Focus (50) makes the nuke weak.
- **No mobility:** no dash, can't chase; Vesper Vale kites her.
- **One stun per 8 turns, melee range:** has to reach the target first.
- **Altar locks are her only tribute counter, and enemies know it.**

**Best moment:** Carrying a big bag to an altar while the enemy's stunners can do nothing.

**Needs:** Strength and Health shards (Durability emblem). Ranged allies for damage.

**Good with:** Ranged damage (Stellar, Tavi) to cover her low output; our own stunners while she ignores theirs.

**Bad against:** Kiters (Vesper Vale); pure damage stacked on her (Stellar + Vendetta); altar locks.

**Draft value:** Strong and flexible. First pick for safety, **and a counter-pick if the enemy's earlier picks are stunners** (picks are revealed each round, P18).

**Art of War angle:**
- P3: make yourself unbeatable first; the safest carrier.
- P18: adapt; counter-pick in the draft.
- P5: know the enemy; her value depends on their stuns.
