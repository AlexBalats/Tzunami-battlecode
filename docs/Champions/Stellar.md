# Stellar

![Stellar](<Stellar.png>)

## Facts (from `GameRules.md`)

A ranged spellcaster who chooses between concentrated damage and an area hit.
- **Slot 0, Single-target damage:** 2 × Focus damage. *(Target / range: Enemy or camp · 4. Walking: Excludes walking. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Slot 1, Area damage:** 1.4 × Focus damage in radius one. *(Target / range: Ground · 4. Walking: Excludes walking. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Stats:** 750 HP / 35 Str / 100 Focus. Per shard: +75 / +3.5 / +10
- **Type:** Ranged, range 3

## Our take

**Role (one word):** Nuker

### Key numbers (base stats, before items)

- Damage per 8-turn cycle **[rules-derived]**: 35 × 8 + 200 + 140 = 620, **~77/turn**. Best of the ranged single champions; only Vendetta (~79) beats it.
- One-turn burst: nuke + basic attack ≈ 235, highest single-turn burst outside Tavi.
- Scaling: a Focus shard adds 20 + 14 = 34 per cycle (~4.25/turn), beating a Strength shard (3.5). **Buy Focus.** 5 Focus shards → nuke 300, area 210.
- Solo wolf **[estimate]**: ~18 turns, takes ~350 of 750 HP. Good farmer; better on camp groups (area spell hits several).

**Strengths:**
- **High, predictable damage** at range 4, outranging every basic attack. Kill maths is easy (P1).
- **Area spell:** best camp-group clearer among the ranged champions.
- **Finisher:** 235 burst picks off low-HP gem carriers (P8, P15).

**Weaknesses:**
- **No crowd control:** can't cancel channels or stop a diver.
- **No mobility, and both spells exclude walking:** a stationary caster.
- **Fragile:** 750 HP.
- **Damage bunched into two casts per 8 turns:** a wasted nuke or escaped target leaves her at 35/turn until cooldown.

**Best moment:** An ally stuns a gem carrier and Stellar deletes them with nuke, area spell and basic attacks.

**Needs:** Focus shards; a frontline; a stunner to set up the nuke.

**Good with:** Stunners (Cassi, Ryth, Tavi); a tank in front.

**Bad against:** Divers (Vendetta, Marcella, Issac); kiters at range (Vesper Vale); Tavi's stun-nuke.

**Draft value:** Strong and flexible, first-pick candidate. Farms and fights well, fits most teams.

**Art of War angle:**
- P1: calculate before you fight; predictable damage makes kill maths easy.
- P8: strike the weak (low-HP gem carriers).
- P15: patience, then decisive strike; save the nuke for the kill.
