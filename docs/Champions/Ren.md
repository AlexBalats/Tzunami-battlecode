# Ren

![Ren](<Ren.png>)

## Facts (from `GameRules.md`)

A melee champion with retaliation damage, a targeted stun and a short dash.
- **Passive:** Retaliates against an attacker for 0.2 × Focus damage when damaged. Ren can still trigger retaliation as he dies.
- **Slot 0, Damage and stun:** 1.25 × Focus damage and a one-round stun. *(Target / range: Enemy or camp · 4. Walking: Excludes walking. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Slot 1, Dash:** Move up to 2 steps, including diagonals. *(Target / range: Ground · 2 tiles. Walking: Compatible. Cooldown: 8 owner turn ends)*
- **Stats:** 900 HP / 40 Str / 100 Focus. Per shard: +100 / +5 / +10
- **Type:** Melee, range 1

## Our take

**Role (one word):** Skirmisher

### Key numbers (base stats, before items)

- Damage per 8-turn cycle **[rules-derived]**: 40 × 8 + 125 = 445, **~56/turn** before retaliation.
- Retaliation is **per hit**: punishes frequent attackers. Vendetta's 9 hits per cycle send back ~180; a nuker hitting once gets 20.
- On camps (**if camp attacks trigger retaliation**): a wolf hits every turn, so ~76/turn. Solo wolf ≈ 18 turns, ~360 HP lost. Camp groups give one retaliation per attacker.
- Scaling: Focus shard = +12.5 nuke per cycle and +2 per retaliation hit; Strength shard = +5/turn. Roughly even; Focus improves the more he's hit.

**Strengths:**
- **Only melee champion with a ranged stun *and* a dash:** stun from 4 tiles to cancel a tribute / recall, then dash in. Engages or escapes on his terms (P14).
- **Punishes basic-attack champions** (Vendetta, Varg, Issac), and possibly camps.
- **Self-sufficient farmer** if retaliation works on camps.
- **Even trades:** retaliation still fires on the killing blow.

**Weaknesses:**
- **Low basic-attack damage** (40).
- **Retaliation is small (20)** and useless vs spell casters (Stellar, Tavi, Prism).
- **Mid HP (900)** with no sustain.
- **Marcella is immune** to his stun.
- **Average at everything:** nothing he's best at except dash + ranged stun.

**Best moment:** Stunning an enemy mid-tribute from 4 tiles, dashing in, and punishing them for fighting back.

**Needs:** Mixed Focus / Strength shards; follow-up damage from allies.

**Good with:** Follow-up damage (Stellar, Vendetta); Tavi (two stuns = 2-round lock).

**Bad against:** Spell casters (Stellar, Tavi) who avoid retaliation; Marcella; kiting at range (Vesper Vale).

**Good against:** Basic-attack champions (Vendetta, Varg, Issac).

**Draft value:** Flexible all-rounder, solid mid-draft pick. Counter-pick into basic-attack teams.

**Art of War angle:**
- P14: seize the initiative; he chooses the engagement.
- P3: make yourself unbeatable first; attacking him costs the attacker.
- P18: adapt; counter-pick.
