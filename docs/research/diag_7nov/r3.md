# r3: redesigning the holder's turn at a contested group (next66 R3), 8 Nov

Code: `bots/_r3` (a copy of `bots/tz` = tz-66 taken at 05:10; no `.cambc`). Everything is behind `value::P.r3`
(default 0). With `r3=0` the bot plays tz-66 exactly: B0–B3 reproduce tz-66's local numbers, and the idle runs match
to the gem. No uploads. `bots/tz` is untouched.

Tags: [measured] = local judge or decoded local replay; [code] = read in `bots/_r3`; [guess] = inference.

**Note: `bots/tz` has moved on since the copy.** It now has `ryth_stun`, `regb_gold` and `gold_draft`, all default
off. `scratchpad/r3/r3_vs_tz_now.diff` shows those as `-` hunks; keep them when porting.

## 0. Answer

1. **Built as specified, the holder redesign barely moves the contact fight. Holds are too small a share of contact
   turns.** [measured]
   - A per-turn count over 4 games v v93 (r ≤ 600) splits our contact turns (an enemy within 5) as follows: **48 %
     break-offs, 29 % fights, 30 % WORK holds.**
   - 61 % of the break-offs are at ≥ 50 % HP, and 45 % come when our local power ratio is ≥ 1.
   - Only ~20 % of the break-offs are by a body at its own live group. The rest are walking in, or elsewhere.
   - On the turns where R3 acts (a hold with an enemy within 6), the champion is in reach only ~22 % of the time.
   - The spec-only build (`r3=1`, mask 31) is level v v93: **−0.1 ± 1.5** on 4 seeds.
   - Its mechanism KPIs move little: champion hits per contact are flat (23.9 → 23.8). Our deaths per contact fall
     (0.37 → 0.30) and hits absorbed per body lost rise (56 → 70).
   - But kills fall (7.7 → 7.2 a game) and the bag exchange drops by 17 gems. **Bit 8 (no assist commit for holders)
     causes that** (§3).
2. **Two additions make R3 matter** [measured]:
   - **bit 32, walk-in hold:** the hold radius grows from 4 to 7, so bodies walking in to their group hold instead
     of breaking off at first sight;
   - **bit 64, commit when winning:** a holder whose local fight the exchange sim wins commits (act_fight) instead
     of holding. Holds are kept for the fights the body would otherwise leave.
3. **Best builds:**

   | Build | Mask | v v93 (4 seeds, paired v tz-66) | Bag exchange | Kills a game | Champion hits per contact | v bandlike (2 seeds) |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | **J** | `r3=1 r3_mask=117` (no power stop, assists on) | **+1.7 ± 1.5** | **+10** (−1.8 → +8.6) | +1.2 (7.7 → 8.9) | **+18 %** (23.9 → 28.2; parity 27.3 → 31.6) | +2.3 ± 1.0 |
   | **HZ** | `r3=1 r3_mask=127 break_closing=1` | **+2.1 ± 1.5**; held maps **+9.8 ± 2.3** | −9 | +0.8 | +12 % | +4.3 ± 1.3 |

   - v bandlike, `break_closing=1` alone gives +4.9. R3 adds nothing on top of it there.
   - v v93, HZ beats break_closing alone by +1.9 ± 1.5 (held +8.0 ± 2.8).
4. **Targets:**
   - The parity hit target (≥ 25) is **already met locally by tz-66** (27.3 v v93): v93 is not the band. So only
     the deltas mean anything.
   - Hits per kill stays at 46–57 v v93 against a target of ≤ 45. v bandlike it is 32–45.
   - Contact take does not move (51 % → 49–51 %).
   - **None of these settings is shown to fix the band contact gap.** It needs the band stage.
5. **Settings for the real-opponent stage:** **J (`r3=1 r3_mask=117`)** and **HZ (`r3=1 r3_mask=127
   break_closing=1`)**, both on tz-66 defaults (§5).

## 1. Design (`bots/_r3/src`)

Parameters are in `value.h`, around l.160–181, after `hunt_brk_r`. The defaults are `r3 0`, `r3_mask 31`, `r3_r 6`,
`r3_out_k 1.3`, `r3_team 0.5`, `r3_ext 3`, `r3_dbg 0`.

An **R3 turn** happens when a body is in a WORK hold (`work_hold`), its stance is NONE, there is no fed break, and an
enemy champion is within `r3_r` 6. In that case the FARM goal calls `r3_farm` instead of `act_farm`.

| Bit | Part | What it does | Where (bot.cpp) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **strike** | Picks the target with `r3_target`: the enemy within reach + 1 with the least effective HP, where effective HP = HP ÷ (our damage a round that reaches it now: every visible ally within its reach + 1). An ally's FIGHT target counts at ×`r3_team` 0.5 and a stunned one at ×0.7; if none is in reach + 1, it looks within 5 for casts. `r3_strike` then scores 9 tiles in order: not lethal > hits the target > hits any champion > (bit 4: fewer enemies reaching, less damage) > stay > nearer the group. The body may only move to tiles within field distance `role_work_r` 4 of the group, or nearer it; guards never step onto capture tiles. A ranged body that already hits from its tile keeps it (two shots: attack + reset + attack) unless a step is clearly safer (fewer enemies reach it, ≤ `kite_gain` × the damage); otherwise it uses step-reset-shoot. Then: stun first when `stun_target`'s rule holds, else `cast_at` the target, then `swing`. With no basic hit available, a cast that reaches (Marcella 4, Cassi's stun 5) still goes to the champion, and the basic attack and the move go to the farm. The capture taker keeps its tile (`act_farm`, then a strike without moving). The melee free hit and the end-of-turn swing use the same target rule. | `r3_reach` l.3183, `r3_ally_list` l.3202, `r3_target` l.3215, `r3_strike` l.3235, `r3_farm` l.3350; free hit l.3786; end swing l.4473; switch l.4424 |
| 2 | power stop | Replaces `work_out_k`. The hold ends when (ΣHP)(Σdamage) of the enemies within 6 ≥ `r3_out_k` 1.3 × ours within 6 (current HP; enemy damage × `enemy_dps_k`). | l.3848 |
| 4 | safe tile | Among the tiles that hit, picks the one fewest enemies reach (a step + basic reach, or a ready spell). `r3_camp_step`: a melee body that hit a camp steps to a tile still next to a live camp of the group that fewer enemies reach. | `r3_strike`, `r3_camp_step` l.3320 |
| 8 | stay | A holder takes no assist commit. | assist condition l.4116 |
| 16 | keep casts | `cast_at` refuses camp targets during an R3 turn, so the casts are kept for champions. | l.1982 |
| 32 | walk-in hold | The `g_working` radius is `role_work_r` + `r3_ext` 3, so bodies walking in hold under the same strict stops. | l.3832 |
| 64 | commit when winning | A holder whose local sim wins (`L.sim_win` or `quick_kill`, a target in sight, HP ≥ `stance_low`) is not forced to NONE. The normal stance logic commits it, and act_fight focuses and closes. If the gate vetoes, the body returns to the hold (p1 bit 4). | l.3888 |

The p3 taker logic and all strict stops are kept: HP < `work_hp`, fed within reach + 2, damage while outsized, the
gate, executes, and `work_bag_stop`.

`r3_dbg=1` prints per-body counters at r600. They are default off, and that is how the turn-share counts below were
measured.

- a contact is a group cycle both sides reach (within 2), r ≤ 600;

- hits per kill = our champion hits ÷ their deaths within 6;

## 5. Recommendation for the real-opponent (band) stage

Both settings go on tz-66 defaults, and both are default-off switches in `bots/_r3`.

1. **J: `r3=1 r3_mask=117`.**
   - **What it is:** strike + safe tile + keep casts + walk-in hold + commit when winning; old out-size stop; assists
     kept.
   - **Why:** the best kill / bag exchange (+10 v v93), the most champion hits per contact (+18 %, parity 31.6) and
     +1.7 ± 1.5 v v93.
   - **Weakness:** no held-map gain (+0.6). On bandlike our deaths per contact rose (0.15 → 0.27), although we still
     won +2.3.
2. **HZ: `r3=1 r3_mask=127 break_closing=1`.**
   - **What it is:** the full R3 (adds the power stop and "stay") plus tz's existing `break_closing` (break off only
     from an enemy closing, within its reach + 1, or within its spell reach).
   - **Why:** the best v v93 (+2.1 ± 1.5, held +9.8 ± 2.3) and bandlike +4.3.
   - **Weakness:** the exchange is worse than tz-66's (−9).
   - **It mixes two levers.** On bandlike, `break_closing` alone does as well. On v93, R3 adds +1.9 on top of it.

**How to judge them on the band:**
- Use contact take and champion hits per contact at parity (`kpi.py` works on band labels too: it reads `cyc_` from
  next66 / transfer, or from `cycles.py LABEL`), plus the r600 exchange and the final margin. Not wins alone.
- If J's extra kills come with a higher band death rate (as on bandlike), stop there. Don't pursue J's direction.
- **The bigger untouched lever is the break-off of non-holders** (48 % of contact turns, 61 % of them at ≥ 50 %
  HP). A redesign of the contact stance belongs there, not in the holder's turn [measured share; the value is a
  guess].

## 6. Caveats

- v93 and bandlike both lose contacts by themselves. Locally the parity-hit target was met before any change, so
  only directions transfer.
- **Mechanism KPIs are one seed and one side (128 games).** At "all" level expect about ±1.5 hits per contact and
  ±0.03 deaths per contact [guess from the bucket sizes]. The parity rows are noisier.
- `fuelEx` rises by 1–4 turns a game with R3 (12.5 → 13–17). Most of that came before `r3_target` cached ally
  reads. After the cache: J 13.2 v 12.5.
- F0 ran before the ally cache; the other R3 runs ran after it. Behaviour is the same except for fuel.
- 2 of the 4 seeds for some rows (ablations, bandlike) are the same seed pairs. Their SEs are about 2.2, so single
  ablation Δs under ±4 are within noise.
